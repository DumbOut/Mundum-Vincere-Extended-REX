{Bombasticus_Maximus_01}
Romans!  Senators!  Friends!

Latium is firmly under the rule of the Senate and People of Rome! 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_03}
The Latin people are now one! 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_04}
Not since noble Brutus drove out the last King, have we known such a glorious day.

{Bombasticus_Maximus_05}
But now, my friends, in this hour of glory, we must be on our guard. 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_06}
To the south, treacherous Greeks and Samnites block our trade!

{Bombasticus_Maximus_07}
Tarquinii groans beneath the tyrant’s heel!  

{Bombasticus_Maximus_08}
That tyrant threatens Rome! 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_09}
Will we abandon Tarquinii? 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_10}
And now barbarians raid from the north. Burning and pillaging roman lands! 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_11}
Shall we let danger fester and grow? 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_12}
Or will we act as Romans? With courage and dignity. 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_13}
Remember. 

{Bombasticus_Maximus_14}
We are the Sons of Mars!

{Bombasticus_Maximus_15}
As senate leader I call upon Tribunes Gaius Julius and Decius Brutus to smash the Gauls and relieve Tarquinii.

{Bombasticus_Maximus_16}
All those in favour say I.

{Julii_Leader_01}
Gods! I hate Gauls. 

{Julii_Leader_02}
My grandfather hated them too. Even before they put out his eyes. 

{Julii_Leader_03}
Did you think I’d be out here on the frontier without a good reason? 

{Julii_Leader_04}
Yes - Rome needs a strong frontier. 

{Julii_Leader_05}
No - Rome doesn’t need unwashed barbarians at her gates. 

{Julii_Leader_06}

{Julii_Leader_07}
Revenge. That’ll be good too. 

{Julii_Leader_08}
This war against the Gauls won’t last long. 

{Julii_Leader_09}
When it’s done, I’ve got plans. 

{Julii_Leader_10}

{Julii_Leader_11}
The Senate. The Greeks. Those Carthaginian elephant riders. The Scipii and the Brutii families too.

{Julii_Leader_12}
After all, the man who controls Rome rules the world! 

{Julii_Leader_13}
And one day, I will be Emperor...

{Brutii_Senator_01}
We Brutii are the only true Romans. We saved Rome. We drove out the kings. We made the Republic.

{Brutii_Senator_02}
The family deserve respect for that. 

{Brutii_Senator_03}
Respect.

{Brutii_Senator_04}
And obedience. 

{Brutii_Senator_05}
WE know what is best for Rome. 

{Brutii_Senator_06}
New lands. Living space. Territory. Slaves. 

{Brutii_Senator_07}
I know what must be done. 

{Brutii_Senator_08}
The Greeks. They look down their perfumed noses at all Romans - and they hate us.

{Brutii_Senator_09}
I’m going to give them a reason for hate. When I’ve CRUSHED them. 

{Brutii_Senator_10}
Roman steel, that’s the answer.

{Brutii_Senator_11}
Roman steel in the Brutii fist.

{Brutii_Senator_12}
And the other great Roman families?

{Brutii_Senator_13}
The Scipii - trash. They have no respect. For proper Roman ways. For us. 

{Brutii_Senator_14}
The Julii prostitute themselves! As if “the people” mattered. Bah!

{Brutii_Senator_15}
We Brutii MUST lead Rome... 

{Scipii_Politician_01}
My family – the house of Scipii – are beloved of the Gods.

{Scipii_Politician_02}
A proud boast but true all the same. 

{Scipii_Politician_03}
In return, we have served Rome, ruled well - led her armies to glory!

{Scipii_Politician_04}
It has cost us dearly, despite the love of the Gods. 

{Scipii_Politician_05}
Sometimes, the hatred of men is stronger! 

{Scipii_Politician_06}
Our dead lie in many graves, put there by Carthaginian swords - and a few Greek ones.

{Scipii_Politician_07}
Even Roman blades have taken Scipii lives. THAT we do not forget – or forgive.

{Scipii_Politician_08}
So, now our time has come. The spirits of the dead cry out for blood!

{Scipii_Politician_09}
I will lead our family in this undertaking.

{Scipii_Politician_10}
The Gods will grant us vengeance! 

{Scipii_Politician_11}
When Sicily is Roman – when Carthage is crushed – when the other Roman families are gone - when the world is mine!

{Scipii_Politician_12}
Then I will stand before the Gods and be worthy of their love.

{Scipii_Politician_13}
And worthy to rule Rome!

{Old_Barbarian_01}
Before my grandfather’s grandfather was born, this was our land. 

{Old_Barbarian_02}
These are good places. Our Gods live here, in the trees and rivers. They watch over us.

{Old_Barbarian_03}
We are happy. We hunt. We love. We have families. Homes. A good life.

{Old_Barbarian_04}
But sometimes, we must fight.

{Old_Barbarian_05}
The Romans disturb the Gods.

{Old_Barbarian_06}

{Old_Barbarian_09}
They take our sons and turn them into little Romans! 

{Old_Barbarian_10}
Ha!

{Old_Barbarian_11}
So we fight to keep what is ours, what must STAY ours! 

{Old_Barbarian_12}
There can be no peace. No peace with Romans - men of stone and iron and lies. 

{Old_Barbarian_13}
There can be only war.

{Greek_Strategos_01}
The Greeks could rule the world.

Alexander did.

{Greek_Strategos_04}

Bring order where there is chaos.

Remake the world of men to a better plan. 

{Greek_Strategos_16}
Perhaps...

I have been in the service of Pharaoh for many, many years. 

{Egyptian_Scribe_02}

{Egyptian_Scribe_03}
We have faithfully recorded and seen much.

{Egyptian_Scribe_05}
All of Egypt lives and dies by His divine wish.

{Egyptian_Scribe_06}
And this is as it should be.

{Egyptian_Scribe_07}
We are his children. Pharaoh loves us all.

{Egyptian_Scribe_08}
We would conquer the world, so that all can know His wise rule.

{Egyptian_Scribe_09}
Even if Pharaoh did not ask this, we would do it. 

{Egyptian_Scribe_10}
His enemies will be struck down. 

{Egyptian_Scribe_11}
Night and darkness will take them. 

{Egyptian_Scribe_12}
The sands will be stained red. 

{Egyptian_Scribe_13}
All this will come to pass, for Pharaoh has decreed it. 

{Egyptian_Scribe_14}
So it is written.

{Egyptian_Scribe_15}
So shall it be. 

{Carthaginian_Seer_01}
Last night, the crying of the children kept me awake. 

{Carthaginian_Seer_02}
And I had a terrible vision.

{Carthaginian_Seer_03}
I saw the fall of our city. Bleached bones under a harsh sun.

{Carthaginian_Seer_04}
Carthage - gone!

{Carthaginian_Seer_05}
Why would Baal send such a vision? He is not cruel. 

{Carthaginian_Seer_06}
He has watched over us. 

{Carthaginian_Seer_07}
We have had victories aplenty in war.

{Carthaginian_Seer_08}
Our merchants sail to all corners of the world. 

{Carthaginian_Seer_09}
And yet now - I fear. I cannot help it. 

{Carthaginian_Seer_10}
We are the envy of lesser people. They tell terrible lies about us. 

{Carthaginian_Seer_11}
They do not understand, so they lie. But the Romans - they are the MASTERS of falsehood! 

{Carthaginian_Seer_12}
War will come. I’m sure of it.

{Carthaginian_Seer_13}
So I will have no more false visions. And...

{Carthaginian_Seer_14}
...I think the children will be quiet tonight. 

{Desert_Warrior_01}
The desert is ours. It is my home.

{Desert_Warrior_02}
Invaders come here, but they do not leave.

{Desert_Warrior_03}

{Desert_Warrior_04}

{Desert_Warrior_05}

{Desert_Warrior_06}
And we grow stronger.

But then, wealth flows through this land.

{Desert_Warrior_11}

{Desert_Warrior_12}
For any sword can be turned aside with a gold coin.

{Desert_Warrior_13}
Gold will buy a thousand warriors. 

{Desert_Warrior_14}
