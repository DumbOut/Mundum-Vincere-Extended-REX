Colors:

white: 235 235 235
grey: 90 90 90

gold: 240 210 90
brown: 128 82 24

1. Take an icon, fill it completely with white or whatever pixels
2. Open it up in GIMP and shrink selection  (6-12 pixels for 256 based symbols, 25-30 for 1024)
3. Crop to selection for the logo, then upsize canvas (8 for 256, 24 for 24) with symbol centered
4. For regular gold icon, resize to 90x90
5. For white positive-negative icons, resize to 40x40, then upsize canvas to 46x46 with icon origin at top left
6. Copy-paste provided positive & negative arrows into the bottom right corner

Use the python files for proper formatting so it's consistent with the vanilla pips.

If it's a logo from Pharoah, before doing anything, crop to the logo's selection then resize canvas so it's about 10 pixels from the sides or 21 pixels from the top/bottom to the logo itself
Then use the primary color for the gold and the outline / detail lines for the brown. Don't just make it all one color.
