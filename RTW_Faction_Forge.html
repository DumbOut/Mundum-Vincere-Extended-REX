Help & RTW Notes

Workflow: Load atlas → pick a channel → paint/wand/auto-segment its zone → repeat for all materials → create factions & set palettes → preview → Export all.

Recolor modes: Fabric imposes hue+saturation, keeps shading (cloth/dye). Metal keeps highlights & shadow contrast, tints mid-tones (bronze↔steel↔gold). Skin/Hair blends gently. Tint multiplies the source by the target color.

RTW format: Classic Rome unit textures are usually DXT3 with a full mipmap chain; the engine references a .tga name while the file is .dds — hence name.tga.dds. Keep dimensions as powers of two.

Shortcuts: B brush · E erase · W wand · F fill · I pick · A auto-mask · L lock channel · X solo · Space pan · Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo · [ ] brush size · 1–8 select channel.

Protection & locks: “Protect skin & metal” stops the cloth channels (primary–quaternary) from ever overwriting metal or skin pixels while you paint, wand, or auto-mask — so cloth recolors never bleed onto flesh or armour. Lock 🔒 any channel to freeze it. Refine Grow/Shrink/Smooth cleans up jagged mask edges. Per-channel Saturation and Lightness sliders fine-tune each recolor.

Runs fully offline. Projects save to your browser and to a .ffproj.json file. Fonts & zip load from CDN on first run.
